I used the learnopengl tutorials as well as the lab notes as reference material, more specifically,
the transformation, the camera class, the model loading and point lighting chapters were very useful.

I load the planet, the 6 cubes, make the cubes self-rotate (unique rotation for each cube), as well as rotate around the light source (planet)

The lighting seems to be a bit out of sync at times. Looks better when the cube self rotation is off.

I have 1 cpp file and 4 shader files, one fragment and one vertex for the planet and one fragment and one vertex for the cubes
